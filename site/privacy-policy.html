Coach Buddy

Privacy Policy

Effective October 18, 2026

Coach Buddy is a soccer coaching app that works entirely offline. It has no accounts, no servers, no ads and no analytics. We don't collect, store or share any of your data.

What the app stores

The teams, players, formations, matches and stats you enter are saved in a database on your phone. That data never leaves your device through the app: there is no server to send it to, and the app doesn't use the internet for your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no sign-in, no tracking, no advertising IDs, no crash reporting and no third-party analytics. We can't see your rosters or match data.

Permissions

The app uses vibration for button feedback. Android also lists network access, because the app framework requires it; the app doesn't send your data anywhere. It doesn't ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

Android device backup

If you turn on Android's own device backup, Android may include the app's data in the backup it saves to your Google account, so it can be restored on a new phone. That backup is run by Google under your Google account settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Deleting your data

Delete a team in the app to remove it with its players and matches, or uninstall the app to delete all of its data from your phone.

Children

Coach Buddy is meant for coaches, not children. The player names and jersey numbers a coach enters stay on the coach's phone and are never sent to us.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
