Coach Buddy

Run your soccer team from the sideline: rosters, drag-and-drop formations, a live match clock, quick subs and every goal and card logged, all on your phone.

  • Teams and rosters with positions, jersey numbers and kit colors.
  • Formations for 5v5, 7v7, 9v9 and 11v11, built by dragging players on a pitch.
  • Live match clock with halves or quarters, stoppage time and one-tap subs.
  • History with results, minutes played and season stats per player.
  • Offline and private. No account, no ads; your data stays on your phone.
