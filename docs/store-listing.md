### Short description (≤80)

```
Rosters, formations, subs and a live match clock for soccer coaches. Offline.
```

### Full description (≤4000)

```
Coach Buddy runs your soccer team from the sideline: build your roster, set up formations by dragging players onto a pitch, then manage the whole match from one screen. It works fully offline, with no account and no ads.

TEAMS AND ROSTERS
• Several teams, each with its own roster, kit colors and field size
• Players with jersey numbers and primary / secondary positions
• Mark players inactive without losing their history

FORMATIONS FOR EVERY AGE GROUP
• 5v5, 7v7, 9v9 and 11v11, with ready-made formations to start from
• Drag players onto the pitch, swap them, and move spots to make your own shape
• Save formations per team and reuse them on match day

MATCH DAY
• Pick who's here, the formation and the starting lineup in a few taps
• Live clock for halves or quarters, with pause, stoppage time and period breaks
• Subs by drag and drop, or one tap with saved quick-sub presets
• Log goals, assists, yellow and red cards; undo the last action
• Big buttons and high-contrast colors made for bright sun and one thumb
• The screen stays awake during the match, and the clock keeps time even if the phone locks

HISTORY AND STATS
• Every result with its timeline and minutes played per player
• Season table: appearances, starts, minutes, goals, assists and cards for each player

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No sign-in, no ads, no tracking
• Your teams and matches are stored only on your phone
```
